# Legal — Lab Files

Lab materials for the Legal track: **BAT Legal Copilot CIE — Hands-on Lab
Guide** (Copilot Apps + Agent Builder, worked in pairs).

> **Synthetic training material.** Lumora Analytics Ltd, every clause, risk
> rating, threshold, policy, person and contact in these files are fictional and
> are not official BAT information, policy or legal advice. Do not use real
> contracts, counterparty information or matter data during the exercises.

**[BAT_Legal_CIE_Lab_Guide.pdf](BAT_Legal_CIE_Lab_Guide.pdf)** — the full lab guide.

## Setup

1. Download the files (use **Code → Download ZIP** on the repository home page).
2. Save a working copy of each file in the training location your facilitator gives you.
3. Keep the four agent knowledge documents together in one OneDrive or SharePoint
   folder if your environment supports folder-based grounding.
