# Sales — Lab Files

Lab materials for the Sales / Customer Management track: **BAT Customer
Management Copilot CIE — Hands-on Lab Guide** (Copilot Apps + Agent Builder,
worked in pairs).

> **Synthetic training material.** Northgate Fuel & Convenience, every account,
> volume, margin, commitment, threshold, person and contact in these files are
> fictional and are not official BAT information, policy or customer data. Do not
> use real customer, pricing or contract data during the exercises.

## Start here

**[BAT_Customer_Management_CIE_Lab_Guide.pdf](BAT_Customer_Management_CIE_Lab_Guide.pdf)** — the full lab guide.

| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Choose two Copilot app exercises (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams) | 20 minutes |
| B | Build the BAT Customer Engagement Guide agent | 25 minutes |
| C | Test and improve the agent | 5 minutes |

## Setup

1. Download the files (use **Code → Download ZIP** on the repository home page).
2. Save a working copy of each file in the training location your facilitator gives you.
3. Keep the four agent knowledge documents together in one OneDrive or SharePoint
   folder if your environment supports folder-based grounding.
