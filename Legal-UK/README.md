# Legal UK — Lab Files

Lab materials for the UK Legal (IP & Patents) track: **BAT Legal UK Copilot Lab
Guide**, run during UK Functional Week.

> **Fictional demo content.** Every patent family, competitor filing, process
> step, turnaround time and contact role in these files is invented for the
> demo and is not real BAT policy, portfolio data or legal advice. Do not use
> real patent, matter or R&D data during the exercises.

## Setup

1. Download the files (use **Code → Download ZIP** on the repository home page).
2. Save a working copy of each file in the training location your facilitator gives you.
3. Keep the four text pages together in one OneDrive or SharePoint folder if your
   environment supports folder-based grounding.
